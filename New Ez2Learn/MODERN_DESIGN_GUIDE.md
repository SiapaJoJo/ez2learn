# Modern Design System for Ez2Learn

## Design Principles
- **Modern Color Palette**: Purple gradients (#667eea, #764ba2), soft backgrounds
- **Glassmorphism**: Frosted glass effects with backdrop blur
- **Smooth Animations**: Cubic-bezier transitions for natural feel
- **Clean Typography**: Inter font family for modern look
- **Card-based Layout**: Elevated cards with subtle shadows
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-first approach

## Color Scheme
- Primary: #667eea (Indigo)
- Secondary: #764ba2 (Purple)
- Accent: #f093fb (Pink)
- Success: #10b981 (Green)
- Background: #f8fafc (Light gray)
- Text: #1e293b (Dark slate)
